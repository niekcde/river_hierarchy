CUNGE PHYSICAL AND CALIBRATION REASSESSMENT — 2026-10-05

CONCLUSIONS
The earlier argument for retaining fixed X on the basis of reversals and a weaker resistance correlation was not justified. Reversals are a sensitivity result, not a failure criterion. Broader predictor performance is mixed. Cunge deserves consideration on physical grounds, but the existing X-only substitution is not equivalent to a complete Muskingum-Cunge implementation.

A previously unseparated implementation effect is material: the RAPID external-inflow boundary applies (a+b)*I_current at the first routing cell. Classical upstream-boundary Muskingum applies a*I_current+b*I_previous. This difference is small at the original X in these tests, but can be enormous for negative Cunge X. It must not be interpreted as hydraulic evidence against Cunge. No production source or notebook was edited for this assessment.

MECHANISM
With K=dx/c, the usual matched diffusivity is
  D_numeric = c*dx*(1/2-X),  D_hydraulic = Q/(2*B*S).
The Cunge X formula matches these quantities. B is water-surface top width, approximated here by routing width under the rectangular-channel assumption. A fixed X=0.1 instead gives D_numeric=0.4*c*dx. Therefore changing X can be a large change in spreading even if median changes in the selected peak metric are small. The calculation matches assumed hydraulic diffusivity; it does not measure real-world diffusivity.

All 80 original selected states were checked for diffusivity matching and exact discrete response moments. The Cunge equality passes at relative tolerance 1e-10. Across the 16 parents, the median ratio of Manning-implied discharge to allocated stored reference discharge ranges approximately 0.236–1.110. The present depth, discharge, n, and celerity caps do not form a uniformly Manning-consistent hydraulic state.

For river 41 / S130_unit_126, saved-event child-minus-parent peak lag changes from +1.210 h (fixed X) to -2.658 h (Cunge reference X). The full impulse-response mean child-minus-parent lag remains +1.558 h. Fixed-X parent/child response standard deviations are 5.11/4.34 h; Cunge values are 59.89/79.78 h. A later mean and earlier maximum are compatible when spreading/skew changes. At fractions 0, .25, .5, .75, 1 of the X change, saved-event peak contrasts are +1.210, -1.515, -2.305, -2.555, -2.658 h. The sign also changes for the controlled 12 h and 48 h pulses. These diagnostics were extended across the six rivers containing the original eight saved-event reversals.

BOUNDARY ABLATION
In engine.py, rte_mat sets q_external=c1+c2; the first cell therefore has a different transfer from an upstream-boundary cell. Its mean includes an extra -K*X-dt/2 relative to the standard discrete upstream transfer. For strongly negative X this extra filtering can dominate the response and does not disappear just by reducing dt.

River 15, stored-reference Cunge X, n=.010, same forcing/geometry/caps:
  Existing RAPID external inlet: peak lag 107.100 h.
  Standard two-time upstream boundary: peak lag 10.247 h.
  Reviewed observed target: 13.0097 h.
At the original calibrated n=.011875 these lags are 117.678 versus 11.569 h. All segment flows remain positive in these ablations. This is a boundary-representation experiment, not a production correction or a claim that lateral inflow should always use an upstream-boundary equation.

For the river-41 reversing pair, the standard boundary retains the reversal: fixed X +1.210 h, Cunge X -2.672 h. Thus that reversal is not created by the RAPID inlet artifact.

INDEPENDENT DIFFUSION-EQUATION BENCHMARK
For the same river-41 parent and four children, solve the constant-coefficient linear advection-diffusion equation on each physical reach using the exact downstream/radiation-boundary Laplace transfer
  H(s)=exp[-2*s*L/(c+sqrt(c^2+4*D*s))].
Use the same c, reference D, branch shares, geometry, forcing, and upstream boundary. This is an independent benchmark for the assumed linear diffusion equation, not a full hydraulic solver or observational validation. The single-reach impulse density integrates to unit mass and yields mean L/c and variance 2*D*L/c^3. Numerical integration checks its transform to 8.9e-15 in the chosen verification cases.

For S130, the saved-event contrast is -1.463 h with hydraulic diffusion versus +1.212 h at the fixed-X nominal diffusion. The reversal persists for both controlled pulses. The 300 s -> 150 s output-grid refinement changes individual peak times by at most 13.45 s across the five states, two diffusivities and three forcings. The ~1.21 h gap between Cunge's -2.672 h and the diffusion benchmark's -1.463 h is material. The direction is compatible with diffusion physics, but quantitative accuracy of this very-negative-X discretization is not established by matching D or by time-step convergence alone.

Long adaptive tails distinguish boundary maxima from resolved peaks. Earlier broad native-inlet searches developed extreme decay tails in several rivers; incomplete native-inlet profiles are retained as partial caches and excluded from full-cohort calibration conclusions. The completed standard-boundary comparison is the primary calibration comparison. A denser .0025 n grid checks the difficult river-11 case. INTERPRETATION
More fits within a defensible roughness band would be useful supporting evidence. A narrower range alone is not a physical objective across heterogeneous rivers. A convincing selection argument needs physically coherent hydraulic inputs and comparable or better prediction of independent events, including hydrograph timing, attenuation and spread. Present n values are effective parameters conditional on the imposed depth/geometry/caps and selected peak definition. With fixed depths and uncapped c proportional to 1/n, Cunge's hydraulic D stays fixed while temporal spreading scales roughly as n^3 in variance; changing n no longer simply stretches the hydrograph in time.

This assessment supports continuing a fair Cunge evaluation and retracting the reversals/correlation-based rejection. It does not justify labelling the current model validated by Cunge, or asserting that an X-only production edit supplies the complete method. The boundary treatment, hydraulic closure and quantitative diffusion accuracy must be addressed explicitly.
