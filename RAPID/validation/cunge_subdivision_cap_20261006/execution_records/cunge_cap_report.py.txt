STABILITY VERSUS POSITIVITY VERSUS ACCURACY
At these parameters N=1 is already bounded. Therefore there is no largest finite alpha that can be selected merely from boundedness.
For O_t=a I_t+b I_(t-1)+d O_(t-1), the impulse weights are h0=a and hj=(b+d*a)*d^(j-1), j>=1. b+d*a=4*K*dt/[dt+2*K*(1-X)]^2 >0. With 0<=d<1, a>=0 ensures positivity for arbitrary nonnegative forcing, even when b<0.
