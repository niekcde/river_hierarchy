Shared representation

integrated into UTC-aligned 60-second (or validation 30-second) interval means.

and explicit forcing endpoint extension flags. No downstream extrapolation.
The initial state remains equilibrium at the original initial forcing value.
A single native clock is shared across selected states and reporting resolutions;
it is an exact divisor of both intervals and respects bounds evaluated AFTER the
beta and spatial subdivision adjustments. Native discharge is audited, not clipped.

engine now supports this convention through output_averaging in its preparation
manifest. Historical manifests retain their original rectangle convention.

Acceptance scope and next stage
This stage integrates and verifies the reviewed base calibration and four example
transitions. It does not claim final observation approval or a completed hierarchy
batch. --diagnostic-gaps is an explicit reproduction option: omission rejects long
upstream gaps. It never turns flagged interpolations into approved observations.
21/22 source timezone ambiguity and local sampling/gap eligibility (notably 55)
remain open. A 60-second report interval does not create 60-second observations.
The reviewed diagnostic post-event tail is retained; full-tail settling and volume
acceptance are still required before final duration/volume hierarchy products.
Full-hierarchy routing is available via --all-states, with the same exterior gates;
this integration has not launched that batch. Every result retains
final_scientific_acceptance=false until these remaining requirements are handled.
No child is fitted, and display normalization does not alter physical routing data.
