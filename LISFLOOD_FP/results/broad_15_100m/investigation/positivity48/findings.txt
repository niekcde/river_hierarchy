Example 48 — conservative positivity and oscillation investigation

Scope: fixed Manning n=0.020799605931143154, same measured widths, bed proxies and normal-flow outlet. Diagnostic copies only; no calibration or production changes. No collapses in this focused failure investigation.

Conservative treatment: compute all proposed signed face fluxes; for each cell sum its outward fluxes, regardless of orientation. Scale all its outgoing faces by min(1, available_volume/(dt*sum_outward_flux)), with a tiny roundoff safety margin when active. Available volume is pre-step water plus positive external source; incoming internal flux is not spent in this step. Each face contributes the identical flux to donor and receiver. The limited flux also becomes momentum history. Deferred-transfer volume is an accumulated change in proposed transfer, NOT water lost.

Focused tests passed: split outflows, reverse flows, outgoing boundary, incoming boundary, chained flow, external inflow, exact previously failing step, analytic uniform Manning equilibrium, inactive-limiter equivalence, lake at rest.

Interpretation: the supplied update admits growing disturbances at representative hydraulics even without the network, outlet or clipping. Grid refinement reduces numerical damping; reach 161 analogue switches from decay at 100 m to growth at 50 m. Reducing timestep does not remove that spatial/model behaviour. This is strong mechanism evidence, not a unique decomposition of every full-network error. The 100 m match can mask instability and should not be treated as demonstrated convergence.

LINEAR CHECK (derived here, not a universal validity threshold): for uniform positive flow, hflow=h_upstream and inactive cap, let r=g*dt*n^2*q/h^(7/3), A=(1-r)/(1+r), C=g*dt*((10/3)*S + h/dx*(1-exp(i*k)))/(1+r), D=dt/dx*(1-exp(-i*k)). The one-step [h,q] perturbation matrix is [[1-D*C,-D*A],[C,A]]. Initializing a tiny eigenmode gives measured amplitude |lambda|^steps. In the corresponding continuum uniform-flow linearization the subcharacteristic condition compares (5/3)u with sqrt(g*h); this is an idealized analytical diagnostic, not an acceptance rule for the real network.
